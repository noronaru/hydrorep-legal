# Questions and answers

## Your data

### Do I need an account?

No. Hydrorep has no sign-in: you open it and start training.

### Where is my data? Does anything leave my phone?

What you log stays on your phone. We have no server and can't see it, and Hydrorep has no permission to use the internet, as its permissions on Google Play show.

Something leaves your phone only through you: your phone's own backup, if it's on, keeps a copy in your Google account (without progress photos), and the files you export or pictures you share go where you send them. Buying Plus goes through Google Play. The [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy) has the details.

### Does Hydrorep use AI?

No. The weekly review and the custom program are worked out on your phone, by fixed rules, and nothing you log is sent to an AI.

### How do I move to a new phone?

Use **Settings → Data → Export data** to save a backup file, then **Import data** on the new phone. If your phone's backup is on, Android can also bring Hydrorep back by itself. Progress photos are in neither; a phone-to-phone transfer that you start yourself takes them along. Plus comes back by itself with the same Google account.

### What if I lose my phone or delete the app?

What's on the phone goes with it, and we have no copy to give you back. Keep a backup file somewhere safe, or keep your phone's backup on.

### How do I delete my data?

**Settings → Data → Reset all data**, or uninstall the app. There is no account to delete, and we hold nothing you logged. Uninstalling doesn't cancel Plus; cancel it in Google Play.

### Can I get my data out?

Yes, always, for free: **Export data** in **Settings → Data** saves a full backup file, and **Export as CSV** writes every set, in the same columns as Strong's export.

### Can I import from Strong or Hevy?

Yes, free: **Settings → Data → Import from Strong or Hevy** reads either app's CSV export. Exercises that clearly match ours are matched; you check the rest, and importing the same file twice adds nothing twice.

## Hydrorep

### What is free?

Everything you need to train: unlimited workouts, programs and exercises, the ten ready-made programs, the rest timer, the plate calculator, supersets, records, this week's and the last four weeks' recaps, the muscle map, exercise charts over 8 weeks and 6 months, body weight, measurements and progress photos, water, today's steps from Health Connect, the home-screen widget, share cards, backup files, CSV export, and importing from Strong or Hevy. There are no ads and no limits on training.

### What is Plus, and what does it cost?

Plus is an optional subscription, monthly or yearly, at the price Google Play shows in the app, in your currency. There is no lifetime option. It adds new things on top:

- Training blocks with a weight and reps to aim for on every set, then a deload, and a report on every block
- Today's readiness, and a lighter day in one tap when it says go easy
- Goals with a date, stalled exercises with what to try next, and weekly set targets per muscle
- The weekly coach, and your own program written around your days, time, kit and goal
- Workouts from your watch, through Health Connect
- All-time exercise charts, comparing two progress photos and every weekly recap
- Your year in lifting, a strength score and long-term stats

### How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Plus belongs to the Google account that bought it, so on a new phone or after reinstalling it comes back by itself. To cancel it, open **Settings → Plus → Manage subscription**, or **Payments & subscriptions** in Google Play. Plus then stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

### How does the streak work?

It counts **weeks, not days**: a week counts when you trained on as many days as that week's plan had, on any days. Rest days never break it, and a week still under way doesn't break the run before it ends.

### How are calories worked out?

They are an estimate from your profile, each workout's time and how hard it felt, not a measurement, so the summary shows them as a footnote.

### Does my watch work with Hydrorep?

Through **Health Connect**, Android's own health store, whichever watch or ring wrote the data: turn on Health Connect sync in your watch's app first. Hydrorep reads your steps, your weight and, with Plus, the runs, walks, rides and swims your watch recorded. A Fitbit or Pixel Watch writes to Health Connect through the **Google Health** app (formerly the Fitbit app), a Galaxy Watch through **Samsung Health**.

### Are there ads?

No, and there never will be.

### Is Hydrorep a medical service?

No. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Calorie figures are estimates. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program.
