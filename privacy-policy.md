# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026

Türkçe: [KVKK Aydınlatma Metni](./aydinlatma-metni)

Hydrorep ("the app") is provided by **Kevser Dinçer Özkurt**, under the brand **Noronaru** ("we", "us"). You can reach us at **noronaru@proton.me**.

## In short

- **You don't need an account.** Hydrorep has no sign-in.
- **What you log stays on your phone.** We have no server, and we can't see it.
- **Hydrorep has no permission to use the internet.** You can check this in its permissions on Google Play.
- **Nothing goes to an AI.** The weekly review and the custom program are worked out on your phone, by fixed rules.
- **No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting tool and no tracking**, and we don't sell anything about you.
- **Two things involve Google**, both under your own Google account: your phone's own backup, if it's on, and buying Plus through Google Play (sections 2 and 4).
- Hydrorep is **not a medical service**.

## 1. What's on your phone

Hydrorep keeps these in its own storage on your phone:

- Your profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level and fitness goal, which the app uses to estimate calories burned and to fit its suggestions to your goal
- Your programs, exercises, exercise notes, weekly schedule, training blocks and strength goals
- Your workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, distances, difficulty ratings and notes, and your personal records
- Your body weight log, body measurements and progress photos
- Your water log
- Your weekly reviews
- Reminder times, your language and other app preferences

**We receive none of this and can't see it.** Everything the app works out from it, including the weekly review, the custom program, readiness and calories, is worked out on your phone. To delete it, use **Settings → Data → Reset all data**, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

## 2. When something leaves your phone

Only in these ways, each your own doing:

- **Android's own backup.** If your phone's backup is on, Android may include Hydrorep's data in your phone's backup to your Google account. This is a Google service under your own account, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings. Progress photos are always left out of it; a phone-to-phone transfer that you start yourself moves them straight to your new phone.
- **Files you create.** When you export a backup or a CSV of your workouts, or share a workout, record or week as a picture, the app creates a file and opens your phone's share menu. Where it goes is your choice, and that service's own terms apply. Importing a backup, or a file from Strong or Hevy, reads it on your phone.
- **Links.** Links in the app (this page, Google Play, RepDB) open in your browser or in Google Play, outside Hydrorep. These pages are hosted on GitHub, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 3. Health Connect (optional)

If you turn on Health Connect in **Settings → Health Connect** and give permission, Hydrorep:

- **writes** each workout you finish from then on as an exercise session — its name, note, start and end time, the kind of workout, the estimated calories burned and, for cardio, the distance — and deletes it there when you delete the workout in Hydrorep;
- **reads** your body weight measurements, to add them to your body weight log;
- **reads** each day's step count, as Health Connect adds up your phone and any watch or app that counts steps, to show on Home;
- with **Plus**, **reads** the cardio workouts other apps wrote there — a watch's, through its phone app (such as Samsung Health) — with their start and end time, kind of workout, distance and calories, and adds the runs, walks, rides and swims you started on purpose (10 minutes or more) to your workout history.

Hydrorep asks for exactly these permissions: write exercise sessions, active calories burned and distance; read weight, steps, exercise sessions, distance and total calories burned. It doesn't ask for your heart rate.

**What Hydrorep reads from Health Connect stays on your phone, and we never receive it.** Weights and a watch's workouts, once in your logs, are kept like the rest of your log, so Android's backup and the files you export (section 2) can include them. Hydrorep's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the [Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134), including its Limited Use requirements. Health Connect data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used for anything other than the features described here. You can turn it off in the app or take back the permissions in Health Connect at any time.

## 4. What we do receive

**Plus, through Google Play.** We sell Plus through Google Play, which takes the payment and keeps the subscription under your Google account. For this, Google is a controller in its own right, under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and we never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play, on your phone, whether your account has Plus, and the answer stays on your phone.

Google Play gives us sales reports (such as the order number, product, price, date and country or region) and can show us limited details of an order when a refund or a payment problem needs handling. We use these for accounting and tax and to handle such problems, and keep them as long as tax and accounting law requires. We can't connect a purchase with what you log, because what you log never reaches us.

**Statistics from Google Play.** Google Play gives us statistics about the app, such as installs, ratings, and crash and performance reports, gathered by Google from phones where sharing that with Google is turned on. They don't include your name, your account or what you log. We use them to keep the app working.

**If you write to us.** If you e-mail us, we receive your address and what you write, and use them only to answer you. Our mailbox is at Proton, in Switzerland. We delete the correspondence once it's no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after the last message.

## 5. What Hydrorep doesn't do

- It has no account, no server of ours and no AI service.
- It shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK and doesn't use your phone's advertising ID.
- It contains no analytics or crash-reporting tool and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't sell personal data.

Workout reminders, rest alerts and the weekly recap are scheduled on your phone, and the home-screen widget is drawn from the app's data on your phone.

To keep Plus out of modified copies of the app, Hydrorep checks on your phone that it is the copy we published. Nothing is sent for this.

## 6. Children

Hydrorep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are under 18, please use the app only with permission from a parent or guardian.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to receive a copy in a portable format, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time.

- **What's on your phone** never reaches us, so we can't find, change or delete it for you, and you don't need us to: you can edit or delete entries in the app, export everything in **Settings → Data**, reset it, or uninstall the app.
- **What we do receive** (section 4): write to **noronaru@proton.me**. We will answer within 30 days, free of charge.

**Legal bases for what we receive.**

- **Sales records:** providing Plus and our tax and accounting duties (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (c); KVKK art. 5(2)(c) and (ç)).
- **Your e-mails:** answering you and handling your request (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (f); KVKK art. 5(2)(c) and (f)).
- **Google Play's statistics:** our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (GDPR art. 6(1)(f); KVKK art. 5(2)(f)).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK); in the EU, the authority where you live or work.

## 8. Security

What you log is kept in the app's private storage and protected by your phone's own security. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so please use a screen lock.

## 9. Not a medical service

Hydrorep does not provide medical advice. Calorie figures are estimates, not measurements, and the weekly review, the custom program and readiness are worked out by rules that can't know everything about you. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, especially if you have a health condition or an injury.

## 10. Changes to this policy

When we update this policy, we change the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is used, the app will let you know in an update.
