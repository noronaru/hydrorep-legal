# Delete your Hydrorep account

Hydrorep is a workout tracker by **Noronaru**. You only have a Hydrorep account if you signed in with Google in **Settings → Account**. If you never did, we hold no data about you: everything is on your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

**One account for Hydrorep, InnerWeather and Dozelight.** Our apps sign in with the same Google account and share one account. Deleting it in any of them deletes it for all of them, including InnerWeather's cloud backup of your journal and the nights Dozelight shares.

**Do you have Plus?** Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (**Settings → Plus → Manage subscription** opens it).

## Delete it in the app

1. Open Hydrorep and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.
3. Choose your Google account once more to confirm it's you.

You need an internet connection. Your account and its data are deleted right away.

## Delete it without the app

Email **noronaru@proton.me** from the email address of your Google account, with the subject **Delete my account**. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We delete your account and its data within **30 days** and let you know when it's done.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account: the email address, name and account identifier Google sign-in gave us
- Your cloud backup of your training log
- Every daily training and water summary Hydrorep shared with InnerWeather and Dozelight
- Everything InnerWeather and Dozelight keep in the same account, such as InnerWeather's cloud backup of your journal
- What our server keeps on your account: your subscription record at RevenueCat, the AI coach's usage counts and any reports you sent

## What is kept

- **What's on your phone.** Your workouts, programs, body weight, measurements, photos and profile stay on your phone. To delete them, use **Settings → Data → Reset all data** or uninstall the app.
- **Files you exported**, wherever you saved them.
- **Workouts Hydrorep wrote to Health Connect.** Delete them in Health Connect, or delete the workouts in Hydrorep while Health Connect is on.
- **Purchases** stay on record with Google Play under its own terms.

We don't keep a copy of deleted data. Google, which hosts our database, may take some time to clear it from its own backup systems.
